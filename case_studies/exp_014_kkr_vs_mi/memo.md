If he misses, KKR become pace-heavy with Narine as the lone spinner, and the band shifts toward its lower end (~53-58% KKR). This single decision, expected at the toss, represents a ~5-8pp swing. Secondary to this: rain interruption could produce a no-result or DLS-affected outcome that introduces massive variance against the favored team.
