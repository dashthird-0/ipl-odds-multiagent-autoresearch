### What the memo got wrong

The memo's best estimate of "~58-60% KKR" overlaps with 59.5%. The scorecard recorded 0.595 (59.5%), suggesting Entry 25's directional adjustment was either not transmitted to the final number or was so small (~0.5pp) as to be indistinguishable from midpoint defaulting. The recurring pattern — Entry 25 is identified, the direction is acknowledged, but the magnitude is timid — appeared in Exp 13 (55-57% evidence → 58% estimate) and recurs here.

- **The venue H2H adjustment (+3-5pp for MI) may have been overweighted relative to the current-form disparity.** MI's 7-3 at Eden Gardens spans multiple eras, many with fundamentally different MI squads (peak Rohit-Pollard-Pandya MI vs the 2026 version at 8th place with 4W-8L). Entry 10 correctly identifies persistent venue dynamics, but the current MI team bears minimal resemblance to the MI teams that compiled the 7-3 record: de Kock gone (season-ending injury), Santner gone, Bumrah struggling, and the team has been ravaged by injuries all season. The 50% era discount was applied, but the remaining +3-5pp may still overweight historical dynamics that no longer persist with this squad composition. The H2H adjustment pulled the estimate 3-5pp toward MI and contributed to Entry 25's directional signal — collectively pushing the estimate further from truth in this instance. A current-form-weighted discount (reducing the +3-5pp to +2-3pp when the H2H team is demonstrably weaker than its historical version) would have been more appropriate.

- **The rain risk adjustment (-2 to -3pp for KKR) was calibrated for a heavy favorite but applied to a moderate one.** Rain-induced variance disproportionately hurts the favorite, but the magnitude depends on how heavy the favorite is. At 89.5%, a rain disruption represents massive asymmetric risk. At 59.5%, the asymmetry is modest — a no-result hurts KKR by ~9.5pp of expected value (from 59.5% to 50%), not 39.5pp. The -2 to -3pp adjustment was reasonable as a band-widener but contributed to the pro-MI lean that Entry 25 aggregated. The rain adjustment should have been scaled to the estimated probability, not the discarded market price.

- **Varun Chakravarthy's fitness uncertainty was correctly identified but the expected-value impact (-2 to -4pp) was wide.** The memo assigned 40-50% probability of Varun missing and estimated a 5-8pp conditional impact, yielding -2 to -4pp EV. This is a 2pp range within the estimate — significant for a signal that the memo identified as the "main uncertainty." The Skeptic should have narrowed this: the "limped badly" observation, combined with the hairline toe fracture history and missing both practice sessions, arguably pushes the miss probability above 50%, which would narrow the EV to -3 to -4pp. Tighter uncertainty estimates on the main swing factor would have sharpened the overall estimate.

### What was missed

- **The interaction between Entry 19's positive momentum and Entry 7's motivation neutrality was not explored.** Entry 20 identifies must-win + losing streak as amplifying negative momentum. But the inverse scenario — must-win + winning streak (KKR's 5W-1L) — was not analyzed. Entry 7 correctly assigns 0pp for motivation, but the form trajectory that Entry 19 captured may itself be amplified (not through motivation but through squad confidence and tactical momentum) in a home match where the team needs to win. The memo treated form and motivation as independent factors, which is analytically correct, but the confidence boost from a winning streak that coincides with a do-or-die match may create a psychological state different from a dead-rubber winning streak (like Exp 9's KKR 247/2). This is not an argument for adding a motivation premium but for slightly increasing the form adjustment (+6-9pp rather than +5-8pp) when the trajectory is concurrent with high stakes.

- **The memo did not adequately distinguish between the informational content of MI's 7-3 venue H2H and MI's 25-11 overall H2H.** The overall H2H (25-11, 69.4% MI) includes matches across all venues and eras. The venue H2H (7-3 at Eden Gardens) is a subset. The memo correctly focused on the venue figure per Entry 10, but didn't explore whether the two figures are capturing the same underlying signal (MI's historical dominance over KKR across all conditions) or a venue-specific one (something about Eden Gardens specifically favors MI against KKR). If the venue H2H merely reflects the overall H2H, the +3-5pp adjustment double-counts with any overall H2H consideration. If the venue H2H captures something additional (pitch profile, crowd dynamics specific to KKR's bowling), the adjustment is independent. The memo assumed independence without testing it.

- **The memo flagged MI's batting ceiling (Entry 18, Rickelton SR 185, Rohit SR 170, SKY T20I SR 167+) but did not model KKR's bowling ceiling as a potential neutralizer.** Entry 23 says to distinguish bowling floor from ceiling. KKR's bowling ceiling is significant: Narine (6.83 economy, the best in the squad) has decades of experience at Eden Gardens, and if Varun plays, the spin pair is among the IPL's most threatening. On a surface where middle-overs spin can be decisive, KKR's bowling ceiling could specifically neutralize MI's batting ceiling. The memo assessed MI's batting ceiling (per Entry 18) and KKR's bowling in aggregate but didn't model the specific interaction between KKR's bowling ceiling and MI's batting ceiling — which determines the band's upper bound for KKR.
