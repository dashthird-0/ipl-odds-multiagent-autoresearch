## Flagged Issues

- **Playing XI predictions are universally speculative.** The two most impactful unknowns -- Travis Head's inclusion for SRH and Bethell vs Iyer for RCB -- are areas where journalist outlets actively disagree. No team management quotes exist for either decision. Downstream agents should treat all XI predictions as uncertain until toss-time confirmation.

- **Phil Salt absence is only "probable," not "confirmed."** CricketWinner.com is not a Tier-1 source. While the claim is detailed and specific, no direct RCB franchise statement is quoted. The claim is two days old (May 20). This is the most consequential player availability claim and it lacks the highest tier of sourcing.

- **Nitish Kumar Reddy's recovery is confirmed for a previous match, not this one.** The captain's toss quote is from the PBKS fixture. His selection today is assumed via extrapolation. This is a common journalistic pattern -- recycling a confirmed past fact to imply a current one.

- **Undated sources appear three times:** NTV Telugu/Khelnow (NKR recovery), Khelnow (Bethell dropping), NativePlanet (dew assessment), and ESPNcricinfo preview (RCB away record). The NativePlanet dew claim is potentially from a different month's match, making it stale.

- **No curator or groundsman statements.** All pitch characterizations are journalist assessments or historical data aggregation. The "batting paradise" framing is venue lore, not current-match evidence. The 2026-season stats (avg 196-202) are more useful than generic descriptions but still lack official pitch preparation details.

- **Standings discrepancy flagged by analyst.** The analyst correctly identified a conflict between an internal stats snapshot and web sources. This is good practice, but downstream agents should verify the correct standings before relying on motivation/scenario analysis.

- **CREX statistical precision may be misleading.** Numbers like "71.62% of wickets to pacers" imply database precision but the underlying sample size and methodology are unknown. CREX is a data aggregator, not a primary statistical authority like ESPNcricinfo Statsguru.

- **"0% rain probability" is stated with false precision.** No weather forecast is truly 0%. The Outlook India caveat about afternoon thunderstorms partially contradicts the "0%" headline figure.

- **Speculative framing treated as near-certain in places.** "Bethell LIKELY DROPPED" uses caps emphasis that may convey more certainty than the evidence supports. The underlying source is journalist inference from stats comparison.
