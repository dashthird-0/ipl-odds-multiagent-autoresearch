- **The streak's continuation probability should have been assessed against the specific opponent.** PBKS's 6-match streak was compiled against RR (3rd), GT (4th), SRH (1st), RCB (2nd), and two others. The fixture schedule context — specifically, the quality gap between the streak-causing opponents and the current opponent — should modulate Entry 14's magnitude. Against a top-4 team, -4pp is well-calibrated; against a 10th-place team missing key players, -2 to -3pp would be more appropriate.

The memo assessed the surface as -1pp against PBKS's "power-hitting approach," but failed to consider the asymmetric benefit: PBKS's BOWLING could benefit from the same surface that their BATTING is disadvantaged on. The net surface effect was modeled as one-directional (bad for PBKS batting) when it was two-directional (bad for PBKS batting, good for PBKS spin bowling). Pant, Pooran, and Badoni are "IPL-proven batters suited to a slow surface" (the contrarian case's own language) — they can accumulate and place the ball rather than relying on pure power. But their VALUE is specifically in replacing Marsh/Markram's roles on a surface where Marsh's SR 148.0 would itself have been suppressed. The absence adjustment should have been modulated by venue: Marsh's 563 runs at SR 148.0 are primarily accumulated on batting-friendly surfaces; his value on a 171-175 par surface is lower than his season average implies. The -5pp absence penalty may have been -3 to -4pp when adjusted for venue-specific run value.
