Their current trajectory is worse than LSG's recent trajectory (LSG won their last home match vs RCB on May 7, and the news reports LSG have won 4 matches total this season). - **Verdict:** Overstated if relying on the stale stats snapshot. The full-season gap exists but must be discounted heavily via Entry 14 and Entry 19.
