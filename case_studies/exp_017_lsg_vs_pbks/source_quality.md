## Flagged Issues

- **Heavy reliance on undated sources**: At least 12 of 31 claims rely on sources with no publication date. This is particularly problematic for pitch and weather claims where timeliness matters.
- **Low-tier source dependence**: Cricket Addictor, BJ Sports, Sportsdunia, Sportsmint Media, AllCric, Cricscope, and Nativeplanet are fantasy/preview content mills that frequently recycle each other's content. Claims resting solely on these sources should be treated as speculative.
- **Predicted XI convergence is illusory**: The Analyst notes "multiple sources converging" for predicted XIs, but cricket preview sites routinely copy from each other or from a common upstream source (often Cricbuzz or ESPNcricinfo speculation). Convergence across low-tier sites is not independent corroboration.
- **Pitch statistics without time-period specification**: The spin vs pace economy figures (claim 16) and chasing win rate (claim 19) have no specified time period. These could be all-time IPL, IPL 2026 only, or cherry-picked windows. Downstream agents should not treat these as precise current-season data without verification.
- **Internal contradiction on dew**: The report simultaneously says dew is LOW and that "moderate dew may appear after 8:30 PM." The Analyst resolves this but the underlying evidence is genuinely mixed.
- **No curator comments**: The Analyst transparently notes no curator comments were found. This means all pitch characterization is from journalists and preview sites, not from ground staff or match officials.
- **Markram and Breetzke availability claims are weakly sourced**: Neither has an official team announcement or management quote. Breetzke's source is undated. Downstream agents should treat the number of available overseas players for LSG as uncertain beyond the Marsh confirmation.
- **Lockie Ferguson's status is genuinely unknown**: Three sources cited, two undated, none confirming or denying fitness for tonight. The claim is correctly flagged as uncertain but downstream agents should give this significant weight as a key variable.
