## Flagged Issues

1. **CRITICAL: Stats snapshot is severely stale.** The stats_snapshot.json shows MI at 10 matches (3W-7L, 6 points) and RR at 10 matches (6W-4L, 12 points). The actual current records are MI 13 matches (4W-9L, 8 points) and RR 13 matches (7W-6L, 14 points). The standings table is from approximately matchday 40-50 of the tournament. Any downstream agent pulling from the stats_snapshot directly will have dangerously wrong data.

2. **CRITICAL: Venue statistics are from the wrong era.** The stats_snapshot venue_splits cover 2017-2019 only (23 matches, avg first-innings score 173). The 2026 IPL Wankhede average is 222 — a 28% increase. Toss preference data (51/72 chose to field) and chase win rates (56.5%) from 2017-2019 cannot be reliably projected to 2026 conditions. The impact player rule alone has fundamentally altered scoring patterns. Per Entry 11 and Entry 13, this data should be discarded.

3. **Key player stats show snapshot staleness.** Sooryavanshi: 404 runs (snapshot) vs 579 runs (news). Jurel: 290 vs 420. Archer: 15 wickets vs 18. The snapshot is ~3 matches behind for both teams.

4. **Parag fitness status is contradictory across sources.** RR official site (May 23) says "sidelined with injury." NewsX predicted XI (May 24) includes Parag in starting XI. Same NewsX injury article says "tested out pre-match." The RR official site, being the team's own communication, should be weighted most heavily — Parag is more likely OUT than IN.

5. **Jadeja fitness relies partly on a lower-tier source.** The key Rathour quote comes from Cricket Addictor. While attributed to a named individual, it has not been independently confirmed by ESPNcricinfo or Cricbuzz.

6. **Predicted XI convergence is illusory.** MI sources disagree on Chahar vs Shardul. RR sources disagree significantly (three materially different XIs). This is divergence, not convergence.

7. **No curator quotes.** All pitch characterization is journalist-sourced.

8. **Travel/fatigue section is unsourced.** Claims 42-43 cite no specific source.
