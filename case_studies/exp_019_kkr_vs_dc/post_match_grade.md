3. **The thin market's overpricing of KKR validated Entry 17/28's skepticism.** At $8,268 volume, the Polymarket price of 56.5% KKR likely reflected pre-binary-gate positioning, brand anchoring on KKR's "remarkable turnaround," and insufficient digestion of the May 22 Raghuvanshi injury news. Removing him and applying a fixed -3pp penalty is less accurate than recomputing the trajectory for the current squad. The asymmetric treatment — KKR's trajectory formally decomposed, DC's trajectory relegated to the contrarian case — was the single largest analytical gap. Had DC's trajectory received +1.5-2pp in the base rate, the estimate would have been closer to 53% KKR (47% DC), producing better calibration.
