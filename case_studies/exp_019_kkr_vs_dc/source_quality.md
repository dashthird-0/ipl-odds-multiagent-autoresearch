## Flagged Issues

1. **Pitch No. 5 claim (Claim 11):** Single-source Yahoo Sports preview with no official groundsman or BCCI confirmation. Specific pitch strip selection is typically not announced publicly this early -- treat with caution.

2. **Dew timing precision (Claim 20):** "From approximately the 14th over" is suspiciously specific and likely generic Kolkata dew lore rather than a scientific forecast for this specific evening.

3. **58% chasing stat (Claim 21):** Timeframe not specified. Could be all-time (decades of data) or current season (3 matches -- far too small a sample). Critical distinction for modeling.

4. **Par score 205-215 (Claim 12):** Presented as a range but with only 3 matches this season at the venue (including one where 147/8 was the first-innings total), this is a journalist estimate, not a statistical conclusion.

5. **DC no injuries (Claim 9):** Absence of reporting is not confirmation of fitness. Explicitly unsourced by the analyst.

6. **Predicted XIs (Claims 5, 8):** Both from a single Yahoo Sports preview article. No team management or official source cited. The analyst correctly labels these as speculation.

7. **Varun availability (Claim 4):** The India TV News article is from May 12, making it 12 days stale. The analyst bridges to the present via inference (he played May 16 and May 20), which is reasonable but the "expected for this match" framing lacks a current official source.

8. **Travel/fatigue section (Claims 37-38):** Entirely unsourced. While likely accurate based on publicly available IPL schedule, no citations are provided.

9. **Match 65 contradiction:** The analyst includes evidence that Match 65 at Eden Gardens was a low-scoring affair (147/8 and 148/6), which contradicts the "batting-friendly throughout" narrative (Claim 10). This internal inconsistency is not acknowledged.
