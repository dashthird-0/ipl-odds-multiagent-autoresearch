The Skeptic's independently constructed base rate reached 58% SRH through explicit factor-by-factor decomposition from a neutral 50/50 starting point: +3pp season quality, +3pp toss advantage, +1pp bowling quality, +1pp batting depth, 0pp H2H (Entry 26), 0pp home advantage (neutral venue), 0pp motivation (Entry 7), 0pp Parag fitness (Entry 5). Per the Exp 18 calibration of Entry 8 ("lean TO the base rate, not PAST it"), and given that Entry 22 confirms independent construction, the midpoint targets the base rate of 58% SRH. The 2.5pp gap is modest enough that this does not represent a large contrarian position -- it is a gentle lean above the market, well within the noise band of any single match.
