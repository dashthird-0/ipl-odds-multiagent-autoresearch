## Challenges

### Challenge 1: Rain/Washout Adjustment May Be Overweighted

**Narrative says:** IMD Orange Alert + wttr.in 100% rain → 15-20% washout probability → +7-10pp GT.

**Base rate says:** Not all Orange Alerts produce washouts. IPL has extensive weather infrastructure. Playoff washouts are historically near-zero. AccuWeather is more optimistic. Match needs only 5 overs per side for a result. Super Over attempted before abandonment.

**Verdict:** Directionally SUPPORTED (washout exclusively favors GT) but magnitude highly uncertain. I set at 10% washout — conservative enough to respect AccuWeather's optimism while acknowledging the IMD alert.
