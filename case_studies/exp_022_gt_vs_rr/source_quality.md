## Flagged Issues

1. **Siraj stats are unsourced/internally circular (Claim #20).** The ~17 wickets figure comes from a "Reflection log Entry 23 reference" from a previous experiment, NOT from an external source. Downstream agents should use the stats_snapshot.json figure of 11 wickets (10 matches) and note it is stale.

2. **wttr.in "100% rain probability" deserves skepticism (Claim #21).** Free weather APIs often round aggressively. The IMD Orange Alert (Claim #22) independently confirms severe weather risk — use that as the anchor, treat 100% as "very high" not literal certainty.

3. **Dew data from Eliminator is internally sourced (Claim #36).** The humidity and dew-point figures from May 27 come from the pipeline's previous experiment, not from an external source.

4. **Imprecisely dated sources (Claims #12, #13, #16, #24).** Several stats sources are dated "~May 2026" rather than a specific date. Likely recent but imprecision is noted.

5. **Corrected standings sourcing is vague (Claim #31).** "Multiple articles" not specified. Structurally consistent but imprecise.

6. **H2H count discrepancy (Claim #32).** Stats snapshot says 9 meetings (GT 6, RR 3). News says 10 (GT 7, RR 3). Likely +1 from stale cutoff.

7. **Archer vs Head stat is irrelevant (Claim #40).** Head plays for SRH, not GT. Not applicable to this match.

8. **Conflicting weather sources under-examined.** wttr.in shows 100% rain; AccuWeather says storm "might drift past." This conflict should widen the rain/washout uncertainty.
